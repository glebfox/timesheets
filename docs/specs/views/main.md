# Main view, login, and error pages

Source: `view/main/`, `view/login/`, `menu.xml`, `security/UiMinimalRole.java`, the `localeDisplayName.*` keys of the message bundles

The application frame around every screen, the login page, and the error pages.

## Main view

The frame of the signed-in application: the drawer, with the logo at its top, the menu, and the user menu at its foot, and beside it the work area, with no bar above either. The drawer lies on a backdrop of soft spots of color: in the light theme, as through frosted glass, the brand's mint behind the logo, its blue in the middle, and a faint mint at the bottom; in the dark theme a warm glow, as of a fire, amber rising from the bottom left through an ember, and dark at the top behind the logo; the work area is a plain card, white in the light theme and black in the dark one, inset from the window's edges so the backdrop frames it. Screens open as tabs in the work area; tabs can be dragged, and each offers closing itself, the others, or all. Why there is no bar across the top and the menu toggle is in the work area: [decision 0011](../../decisions/0011-the-main-view-has-no-navigation-bar.md).

- **Logo** — the Timesheets logo without the tagline heads the drawer across its width, the mark lined up with the menu's icons, drawn in the brand's blue in the light theme and in its ivory in the dark one, with the mint corner in both, whether the theme is picked in the user menu or follows the system; its accessible name is "Timesheets", the logo's own title, the same in every language.
- **Menu toggle** — opens and collapses the drawer. It is at the start of the tab bar, before the tabs, and in the same place above the dashboard while no tab is open, on a touch screen too.
- **Dashboard** — while no tab is open, after sign-in and after the last tab closes, the work area shows the [dashboard](dashboard.md) under the menu toggle.
- **Menu** — Timesheets (Calendar, My time entries, My tasks), Project Management (Clients, Projects, Time entries, Approve time entries, with a line between Projects and Time entries), Settings (Task types, Project roles, Work schedules, Holiday calendars, Absence types), and Administration (Users, Absences) start expanded; Security, Data Tools, and Pessimistic locking hold the framework's own screens and start collapsed. Every group and item shows an icon of its own, and no two are alike. Rows are rounded; the current item is a raised white row, in the dark theme with dark text and a badge it holds looking as in the light theme, a hovered row a fainter one, a group holding the current item reads as active even collapsed, and a group's items hang on a line from the group's icon. A user sees only the items their roles allow. The menu scrolls on its own, with a thin scrollbar, while the logo above it and the user menu below it stay in place.
- **Rejected badge** — the My time entries item carries a red badge with the number of the current user's Rejected [time entries](../data-model/time-entry.md#status), hidden when there are none. It refreshes while the user is signed in, whenever one of their entries changes status or is deleted, by anyone and from any session. The dashboard's Needs attention shows the same number, read each time the dashboard is shown.
- **User menu** — at the foot of the drawer, across its width, so while the drawer is collapsed it is reached by opening the drawer. The button shows the user's avatar and full name (the username when both names are empty), cut short when it does not fit, and marks a substituted user. The menu it opens is at least as wide as the button; its header shows the name with the username beneath it. Its items are Substitute user, the theme switch, and Log out.

## Login

`login`. The page every user sees before signing in: the brand beside a sign-in form.

- **Sheet** — on a window at least 1024 pixels wide, a sheet in the brand blue fills the left, 42% of the window but no narrower than 440 pixels and no wider than 720. Its top right corner is cut on the diagonal and folds over in mint, as the top strip of the brand's mark does, and the cut shows the form's side behind it. The Timesheets logo with its tagline, "Time, composed.", drawn for dark surfaces, sits at its bottom left and grows with the sheet; its alternative text is "Timesheets". The blue, the mint, and the logo are the same in both themes, and the tagline stays English on the Russian page.
- **Form** — beside the sheet, on the theme's surface color, a column up to 560 pixels wide is centered in the rest of the window, and the form stands at its left edge, centered vertically, without a card. Its title is "Log in"; below it come username, password, "Remember me", and Log in, and there is no "forgot password". The `dev` profile pre-fills `admin`/`admin`. A failed sign-in shows the form's error, a red-tinted block under the title, without saying why: wrong credentials, an inactive or locked user, and a user without UI access look alike. The button and the focus rings take the app's accent, so in the dark theme, whose accent is a neutral white, the button is white with dark text beside the blue sheet.
- **Language** — a switch of English and Russian, each named in its own language, "English" and «Русский», and marked as being in that language for assistive technology, outside the form: in the top right corner of the form's side. It starts at the first of the browser's preferred languages that the app offers, or at English when the browser prefers neither, and the checked one is in the text color and semibold. Checking a language changes the page's language at once, the title, the labels, the button, the error, the switch's own name, and the browser tab's title, «Вход - Timesheets» in Russian, while what is typed stays, and the session that signs in takes the language checked. To the keyboard it is a radio group named "Language": one Tab stop after the Log in button, with the arrow keys moving between the languages.
- **Narrow windows** — below 1024 pixels the sheet is a band across the top, with the fold at its right and the logo at its bottom, on the form's left edge, or at the band's left on a window too narrow for the form's full 400 pixels: 216 pixels tall on a phone, 280 from 600 pixels wide, and 120 on a window under 500 pixels tall, such as a phone turned sideways, so the form starts on the first screen. The form is under the band, as wide as the page allows up to 400 pixels, centered, and the language switch is centered at the page's foot, under the form; it stays at the window's foot while the page fits and follows the form when the page scrolls.
- **Scrolling** — the page scrolls as a whole when it does not fit the window, and nothing is pinned over anything else; on a short wide window the sheet grows with the page, the logo staying at its bottom.
- **Theme** — the page follows the theme picked last in this browser, or the system's; it has no theme switch of its own.
- **Forced colors** — in a high-contrast mode the sheet's blue gives way to the system's background, the fold disappears, the logo is drawn in the system's text color, and the checked language keeps its weight and takes the highlight color.

## Error pages

An unknown address followed within the application without reloading the page, such as through a link to a screen that no longer exists, shows Vaadin's default page, "Could not navigate to" the address, which in development mode also lists the application's routes. An unknown address typed into the browser never reaches the application: a signed-in user gets the server's plain error page (Spring Boot's Whitelabel Error Page) with status 403, and a signed-out user is sent to the login page. Why such addresses stay forbidden: [decision 0005](../../decisions/0005-addresses-that-open-no-view-stay-forbidden.md).

A screen the user may not open does not open, whether its address is followed or typed into the browser: an "Access denied" notification is shown instead, over the main view when the address was typed.
